# Description

Your parents want to paint your room and asking for your favorite colour. You decide to show them your favorite colour through this HTML page of yours.

Design a static HTML page that showcases your favourite colour.

# Requirements

## UI requirements

1. A text for your favaourite colour.
   - `My favourite colour is <colour-name>.`
   - E.g. `My favourite colour is red.`
2. Colour of the text `<colour-name>` should be same as the mentioned `<colour-name>`.

Please refer to `Sample UI` section below, for an example.

## Other requirements

1. Text must be rendered inside a paragraph tag.
2. `<colour-name>` must be rendered inside a span tag.
3. Span element should contain an attribute `class` and its value should be `favorite-color`.
