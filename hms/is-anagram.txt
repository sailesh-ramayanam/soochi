The problem statement is very simple.
- We have 2 strings.
- We need to check if they are anagrams or not.

To be able to solve this, we need to understand the meaning of anagram.
Before proceeding further, use internet and read about anagrams (Warning: Do not look up the solutions).

Two strings are called anagrams, if one string can be obtained by rearranging the characters of the second string.
